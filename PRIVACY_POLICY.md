# RockyCrypt Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

## Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 2026**

RockyCrypt is a digital market intelligence platform for users interested in the Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE) and broader financial analysis. By creating an account, you consent to the collection, use, storage, and protection of your personal information as described below.

### 1. Information We Collect
- Account data: name, email address, password hash, verification status, and registration timestamp
- Subscription data: plan type, payment status, and billing reference when applicable
- Product usage data: portfolio entries, watchlist items, alert preferences, and pages viewed
- Communication data: emails sent for verification, password reset, reports, and account notifications

### 2. How We Use Your Information
- To create and secure your account
- To verify your identity and protect against abuse
- To provide market analysis, alerts, portfolios, and premium features
- To send operational emails such as verification, reset links, and account updates
- To improve the experience, reliability, and security of the platform

### 3. Data Storage and Protection
- Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes and are never stored in plain text
- Authentication uses signed JWT tokens with expiration
- Sensitive routes are protected by server-side access checks
- Data is stored in local JSON files and protected by access controls and rate limiting
- We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to reduce unauthorized access and misuse

### 4. Sharing and Disclosure
- We do not sell personal data to third parties
- We may share limited information with payment providers or email providers solely to provide subscribed services and operational communications
- Information may be disclosed if required by law or to protect the integrity and safety of the platform

### 5. User Rights and Choices
- You may request account deletion or data correction by contacting the administrator
- You may unsubscribe from marketing or report emails where applicable
- You are responsible for keeping your login credentials secure

### 6. Limitation of Responsibility
While RockyCrypt uses reasonable safeguards, no internet-based system is 100% secure. Users should take precautions to protect their accounts and personal information.
