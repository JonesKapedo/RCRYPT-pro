How It Works
Everything you need to know about RockyCrypt
What is RockyCrypt?
NSE Kenya Trading Intelligence Platform
RockyCrypt is a professional-grade stock analysis platform built specifically for the Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE). It combines real-time market data from the official NSE ticker API with advanced technical analysis to deliver actionable buy/sell signals, screening tools, portfolio tracking, and market intelligence — all in one sleek dashboard.
How Signals Work
The Scoring System
Every stock is scored on a scale of -100 to +100 based on multiple technical indicators. The higher the score, the stronger the bullish signal. The lower the score, the stronger the bearish signal.
1
Data Collection
Live prices are fetched from the official NSE Kenya ticker API every 5 minutes. Historical data is accumulated daily for each of the 70+ tracked stocks.
2
Technical Analysis
Our engine computes RSI (14), MACD, SMA (20 & 50), EMA (12 & 26), Bollinger Bands, Stochastic Oscillator, and volume analysis for each stock with sufficient history.
3
Signal Generation
Each indicator contributes to a composite score. Price above moving averages adds points, oversold RSI adds strong buy points, MACD momentum adds or subtracts, and so on.
4
Signal Classification
Scores map to signals: STRONG BUY (40+), BUY (20-39), LEAN BULLISH (5-19), NEUTRAL (-4 to 4), LEAN BEARISH (-5 to -19), SELL (-20 to -39), STRONG SELL (-40 or below).
Market Coverage
70+ NSE Stocks Across 12 Sectors
We track all major NSE-listed equities including Banking (SCOM, EQTY, KCB, COOP, ABSA, NCBA), Insurance (BRIT, JUB, CIC), Energy (KPLC, KEGN, TOTL), Manufacturing (BAMB, BAT, CARB), and more. Data is sourced directly from the official NSE ticker via deveintapps.com.

Getting Started
1
Load Market Data
Click "Run Analysis Now" on the Dashboard to fetch the latest NSE prices and generate fresh signals. This takes about 10-15 seconds.
2
Browse the Market
Use the Market page to view all tracked stocks, filter by buy/sell signals, search by symbol or sector, and sort by price, change, or volume.
3
Use the Screener
Apply 10+ filters (price range, signal type, score, volume, sector) to find exactly the stocks that match your criteria.

Add your holdings to the Portfolio Tracker to monitor real-time P&L. Use the Watchlist to keep an eye on stocks you're interested in.
Frequently Asked Questions
How fresh is the data?
Live prices are fetched directly from the official NSE Kenya ticker API (deveintapps.com/nseticker/api/v1/ticker) — the same source used by nse.co.ke. Data is cached for 5 minutes to ensure performance. Historical data is accumulated daily and stored locally.
How accurate are the signals?
RockyCrypt signals are based on standard technical analysis indicators (RSI, MACD, Moving Averages, Bollinger Bands, Stochastic) that are widely used by traders. However, no signal is 100% accurate. Always combine signals with your own research and risk management. RockyCrypt does not provide financial advice.
What does "data_days" mean?
Data days refers to the number of trading sessions of historical data available for a particular stock. Full technical analysis requires at least 20 days of data (for SMA20 and Bollinger Bands) and ideally 26+ days (for MACD). Stocks with fewer than 20 days use a simplified scoring system based on daily change and range position.
Can I use RockyCrypt on mobile?
Yes! RockyCrypt is fully responsive and works on all devices. The mobile interface includes a bottom navigation bar, touch-friendly controls, and an AMOLED-optimized dark theme for the best mobile experience.
How do I upgrade to Pro?
Navigate to the Pro page from the sidebar or bottom nav. Choose your billing period (Monthly, 6 Months, or Annual) and click Subscribe. For now, contact admin@rockycrypt.local to complete your subscription. Pro features include unlimited alerts, arbitrage scanner, bonds & dividends data, heatmap explorer, multi-chart engine, AI insights, and more.
Is my data saved?
All your data (alerts, portfolio, watchlist, settings, recent views) is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. Nothing is sent to any server. Clearing your browser data will remove this information, so we recommend exporting important data via CSV.
